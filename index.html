Who I Am

I'm a Computer Science Engineer who loves building clean, thoughtful software. When I'm not coding, you'll find me behind a camera chasing light and interesting compositions.

This website is where both worlds meet — a space for my professional work and my creative side. Feel free to look around and reach out if something catches your eye.

I specialize in web development, system architecture, and UI/UX design. I believe in writing code that is not only functional but also maintainable and elegant.

On the photography side, I focus on capturing raw, authentic moments, whether that's in the streets, nature, or studio portraits. When I'm not working, I'm usually exploring new technologies, reading about design systems, or planning my next photography trip.
